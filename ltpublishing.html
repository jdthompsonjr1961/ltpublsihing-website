The literary imprint

Books we believe in enough to put our name behind.

No upfront production fee. No package. We read the work, decide whether it belongs here, and enter the relationship as a publishing partner.

What partnership means

We want to read your book—and, when it is right for us, help make it stronger.

LT Publishing is built around close editorial partnership. We are interested in literary work with a strong voice, emotional consequence, and a clear reason to exist.

When we take on a book, we work with the author through editing, production, design, publication, and the life of the finished title. We are not simply providing a service. We are putting the LT Publishing name behind the work.

Submit to LT Publishing

Selection cannot be purchased

02

You see the changes, understand the reasoning, and remain an active participant in decisions about your book.

03

Rights and money are stated plainly

Copyright remains yours. Royalty terms, license scope, reporting, and reversion are set out before anything begins.

04

We do not rush books

Small press means limited capacity, but it also means the book is handled closely rather than passed through a production queue.
