What We Publish

Literary fiction
that earns its ending.

LT Publishing is not a genre imprint and not a commercial press. We publish work that is formally ambitious, emotionally honest, and written with the conviction that readers can do interpretive work without being guided through it.

What we look for

We don't have a formula. But there are qualities we return to — and a submission that carries most of them gets our full attention.

  • Literary fiction with a strong, original voice
  • Work that trusts the reader
  • Stories grounded in specific place and time
  • Emotional honesty without sentimentality
  • Prose that rewards close reading
  • Endings that are earned, not resolved

What we don't publish

Being clear about this saves everyone time. These aren't judgments on quality — they're simply outside the scope of this imprint.

  • Genre fiction (thriller, romance, fantasy, sci-fi)
  • Self-help or prescriptive nonfiction
  • Children's or middle-grade fiction
  • Poetry collections
  • Work that requires significant developmental revision
  • Previously self-published titles

Genre fiction may be a strong fit for LT Select. Production services without imprint consideration are available through LT Media.

If selected, everything is included.

  • Full editorial partnership — developmental and line
  • Signature LT cover design
  • Interior formatting to KDP spec
  • ISBN registered under LT Publishing
  • KDP softback and hardback upload
  • Listed in the LT Publishing catalogue
  • No upfront fee of any kind
  • Royalties shared — terms stated clearly before you sign
The honest version

We are a small imprint. We take on very few manuscripts each year. Selection is not a service we sell and cannot be purchased through LT Media or LT Select. It happens when a manuscript arrives that we cannot stop thinking about.

If that's not your manuscript this time, we'll tell you clearly — and tell you why, if we can.

On Rights

You retain copyright. LT Publishing holds a limited license to produce, distribute, and sell the book through agreed channels. That license can be ended by either party with reasonable notice.

Royalty terms are stated plainly before you sign anything: what percentage you receive, how it's calculated, when it's paid. No vague language, no complex formulas designed to obscure what you actually earn.

If the book goes out of print or sales fall below a defined threshold, rights revert to you automatically. No buyback clauses, no exit fees.

On Process

We respond to every submission. If your manuscript isn't right for LT Publishing, you'll hear that clearly and, where possible, why. We don't leave submissions unanswered.

If selected, the editorial process is collaborative. You see every suggested change. The cover is designed in partnership — you'll have meaningful input, not just approval of a finished file.

We are a small operation in Lisbon. We work carefully and don't rush books. If you need a book produced on a tight timeline, LT Media is the better fit.

LT Publishing · Author Protections

How We Protect Authors

A publishing agreement is a business relationship. These are the terms of every LT Publishing contract — stated plainly, before you sign anything.

Your Copyright — Always

We hold a license to publish your work. You own it. Copyright remains in your name in every edition we produce, and reverts to you fully when the agreement ends.

50% of Net Receipts

You receive fifty percent of net receipts on print, eBook, and audio — on every copy sold, through every channel. Royalty statements quarterly. Audit rights included.

Reserved Rights Stay Yours

Film, television, translation, dramatic, serialization, and anthology rights are not granted to us. We take world English-language print, eBook, and audio. Everything else remains yours to exploit as you choose.

Two-Year Term, Then Yours to Leave

The initial term is two years from first publication. After that, either party may end the agreement with 90 days' written notice. No perpetual lock-in. No penalty for leaving.

Out of Print Means Rights Revert

If your book is unavailable through any sales channel and we fail to remedy that within 90 days of your written request, all rights revert to you immediately. No negotiation required.

Plain Written Terms Before You Sign

Every LT Publishing agreement is in plain English, in writing, before anything begins. No verbal commitments. No terms buried in fine print. You will know exactly what you are signing.

Questions before you submit? Reach us through the submission form. We're happy to answer before you decide — no commitment required.
