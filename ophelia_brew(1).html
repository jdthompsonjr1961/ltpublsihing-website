About the Author

Ophelia Brew is a poet, writer, and first generation Ghanaian American raised in the Bronx. A Howard University graduate who studied criminology and philosophy, Ophelia's writing is deeply inspired by ancestry, migration, faith, and the quiet strength of women.


In Before Greener Pastures, her debut poetry collection, she writes toward a place beyond certainty — a dream of elsewhere — and honors the courage it takes to yearn for more, even when the path is unclear. Her poems live in the in-between spaces: between where she comes from and where she is trying to go, between faith and fear, between survival and becoming.


Before Greener Pastures is her debut poetry collection.
