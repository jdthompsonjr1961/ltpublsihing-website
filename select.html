Selected commercial fiction · practical nonfiction

A publishing partnership with shared commitment.

LT Select is for books we choose to stand behind through a collaborative model in which both the author and the press take on meaningful responsibility.

What LT Select is

Selected, not simply purchased.

LT Select is intended for commercially focused fiction and practical nonfiction that fits the house but does not belong under the literary imprint.

We still choose the books. Acceptance cannot be bought, and not every project is suitable for the list.

01

What we contribute

Editorial judgment, production planning, design, formatting, metadata, platform setup, and an ongoing stake in the quality of the finished book.

02

What the author contributes

Serious revision, timely decisions, participation in production, promotional effort, and a greater share of the practical and financial commitment.

03

How it differs from services

A services client hires us for defined work and keeps the project entirely independent. An LT Select author joins the imprint and enters an ongoing publishing relationship.

04

How rights and costs are handled

Fees, royalties, rights, ownership, reporting, and exit provisions are stated before the agreement is signed. There are no hidden packages or surprise add-ons.

The standard

The production standard does not change.

LT Select books receive the same attention to editing, design, formatting, and presentation expected across the house.

The model is different. The care is not.
