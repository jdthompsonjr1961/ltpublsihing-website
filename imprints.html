The imprints

Three publishing paths. One standard of care.

The right imprint depends on the book, the audience, and the kind of partnership required to bring it into the world properly.

01 · Literary imprint

LT Publishing

LT Publishing is the traditional small-press imprint for literary work we choose to acquire and publish under our own name.

There is no upfront production fee. We invest editorial work, design, production, publication, and our own reputation in the finished book. Selection cannot be purchased.

Learn about LT Publishing

02 · Collaborative publishing

LT Select

LT Select is for commercially focused fiction and practical nonfiction chosen for a more collaborative publishing model.

We contribute editorial judgment, design, production experience, publishing infrastructure, and an ongoing stake in the title. The author takes on a greater share of the practical and financial commitment. The book must still be selected; this is not a way to purchase the LT name.

Learn about LT Select

03 · Art editions

Belle Époque Editions

Belle Époque Editions develops historically grounded art books from carefully sourced public-domain visual collections.

The imprint places artwork, provenance, design history, and responsible reuse at the center of the publication rather than treating archival images as decoration.
