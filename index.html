LT Publishing is an independent imprint based in Lisbon, Portugal. Every book we publish — under any of our imprints — is made with the same attention given to the stories themselves. Cover, type, interior — all of it considered.

LT Select extends that standard to commercial fiction and nonfiction we believe is worth producing well.

We believe in the kind of story that earns its ending.
