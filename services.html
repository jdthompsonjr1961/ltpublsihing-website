Our approach to fees

Useful work at a fair price.

Publishing services should solve a problem, not create a financial crisis. We define the scope, explain what is wrong, and quote the work clearly before beginning.

Most authors do not need a package costing thousands of dollars. They need someone who can identify the problem, fix it properly, and explain what was done.

We do not sell unnecessary add-ons, vague prestige, or promises about sales. We charge for editorial and production work actually performed.
