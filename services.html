How It Works

From Manuscript
to Bookshelf

A straightforward process with no guesswork. You'll know exactly where your book is at every stage.

01
Submit

Send us your manuscript and a brief description. We read everything. You'll hear back within two weeks with either a package quote or, for exceptional work, an imprint conversation.

02
Edit

Your editor works through the manuscript — developmental concerns first, then line by line. You see every suggested change. Nothing is final without your approval.

03
Design

Cover and interior design by a professional with a graduate background in art history. We present options; you choose. The interior is formatted to KDP spec and proofed before upload.

04
Publish

Your ISBN is assigned — either registered to LT Press at no additional charge, or your own ISBN purchased through Bowker (~$125) if you prefer full portability. We upload to KDP, verify the proof copy, and hand you a live book — and complete ownership of everything we produced.

On Ownership

When you engage LT Media as a production client, you own everything: the edited manuscript, the cover files, the ISBN, and all royalties. Our fee is for services rendered, not a stake in your book.

Every LT Media package includes an ISBN registered to LT Press, an LT Media imprint. This costs you nothing and works for all KDP distribution. If you prefer your own ISBN — registered in your name or your own imprint, with full portability to any distributor — we'll walk you through purchasing one at Bowker (~$125 per ISBN). Either way, you retain 100% of your royalties. We never claim a stake in your book.

KDP login credentials remain yours. We are granted temporary upload access only, and that access ends when your project is complete.

On the Imprint

Occasionally a manuscript arrives through LT Media that we want to publish under the LT Publishing imprint rather than simply produce. When that happens, we'll reach out directly.

If you're specifically interested in imprint consideration, visit the LT Publishing page to learn more and submit your work there.

LT Media is a production service. Everything we produce here is made to the same standard as anything bearing our name.

LT Media · Author Protections

How We Protect Authors

LT Media does not take your copyright, your royalties, your ISBN, your KDP account, your files, or your passwords. These are not policies buried in fine print — they are the terms of every engagement, stated plainly before any work begins. Your payment is secured through Escrow.com — held by a licensed third party until you confirm the work is done.

Payments Secured Through Escrow

Every LT Media transaction is processed through Escrow.com, a licensed third-party escrow service. Your payment is held securely before work begins and released only when you confirm the work is complete. The price quoted is the price held — no surprises, no pressure.

No Password Access Required

For most KDP file problems, you send us the rejection message, trim size, page count, and files. You never give us your login credentials. If temporary upload access is needed for a full package, it is revoked the moment your project is complete.

No Fix, No Fee on Diagnostics

For KDP file troubleshooting, if we cannot identify or correct the issue within the agreed scope, there is no charge. We tell you what's wrong and what it costs before we touch anything.

Clear Scope Before Work Begins

You will know what is included, what is not, and what the fee is before any work starts. No hourly guesswork. No surprise invoices. The price you agree to is the price you pay.

You Receive Every File

All deliverables — cover files, formatted interior, production assets — are returned to you for your own KDP or IngramSpark account. You remain the publisher of record. We retain nothing.

100% of Royalties, Always

LT Media is a production service. Our fee covers work performed — not a stake in your book. Every dollar KDP or IngramSpark pays goes to you. No royalty participation of any kind.

No Exclusivity, No Lock-In

Working with LT Media does not prevent you from publishing elsewhere or moving to any other platform. There is no exclusivity clause in any LT Media production agreement.
