Publishing services

Editorial judgment. Professional design. Practical production help.

Support for authors and small publishers who need a manuscript assessed honestly, a book designed properly, or a production problem solved without being pushed into an inflated package.

Specific fix. No result, no charge. For clearly defined production problems, if we cannot fix the issue we agreed to address, there is no charge.

Editorial work

Editing begins with close reading.

Editorial support is not limited to proofreading. We offer manuscript assessment, developmental editing, line editing, continuity review, copy preparation, and final pre-publication review.

LT Publishing’s editorial work is led by an English major with extensive experience in writing, editing, legal reasoning, and complex document production. The aim is to make the book stronger while preserving the author’s voice.

Design

A book should be designed, not assembled.

Cover and interior design are approached as professional design work rather than template placement. Our design work is supported by an experienced graphic designer, with attention to typography, hierarchy, image treatment, print specifications, and the way the finished book functions as a physical object.

Our approach to fees

Useful work at a fair price.

Publishing services should solve a problem, not create a financial crisis. We define the scope, explain what is wrong, and quote the work clearly before beginning.

Most authors do not need a package costing thousands of dollars. They need someone who can identify the problem, fix it properly, and explain what was done.

We do not sell unnecessary add-ons, vague prestige, or promises about sales. We charge for editorial, design, and production work actually performed.

Platforms

Most major publishing platforms.

We can prepare and troubleshoot books for KDP, Lulu, and most other self-publishing and print-on-demand platforms.

IngramSpark is the current exception because of an unresolved account-verification issue, not because of any limitation in production capability.

Working policy

Defined scope. Escrowed payment. Clear handoff.

Client projects are scoped in writing and handled through escrow. You know what will be delivered, what it will cost, and when payment is released.

You retain your manuscript, production files, account control, and royalties. Working with us does not create an ownership stake in your book.

What we will not do

LT Publishing is not a vanity publisher.

You cannot buy your way into publication under one of our imprints.

Hiring us for editing, design, or production services does not guarantee that a book will be accepted by LT Publishing or LT Select. Imprint publication is a separate editorial decision based on the manuscript and its readiness.

If a book needs substantial revision, we will say so. If we are not the right fit, we will say that too. Our role is to help produce a stronger book—not to collect a fee for putting an unfinished one into print.
