About the Book

A friendship that ends
the only way impossible things can.

When a 312-year-old leprechaun arrives in suburban Alabama — exiled from his clan and entirely unprepared for central air conditioning — he is taken in by nine-year-old John Dale Greene, who keeps two notebooks: one for things that happen, one for things that shouldn't be able to.

What follows is a summer of pancakes sneaked upstairs, wheelchair rides past murdered crepe myrtles, a wish gone wrong in Clay County, and a friendship between two people who are both, in their own way, trying to figure out where they belong.

Billy and the Kid is the kind of story that feels like a fable while it's happening and like a grief once it's done. It ends the only way it can — and the only way it should.

What We Ask

This is a real book.
We ask for a real read.

Advance readers are not obligated to love the book — but they are asked to finish it and leave an honest review. We select readers carefully, keep the group small, and send the manuscript only to people who asked for it specifically.

  • Read the full manuscript within four weeks of receiving it
  • Leave an honest review on Amazon, Goodreads, or both — a sentence or two is enough
  • Keep the manuscript private — please do not share or distribute
  • No obligation to be positive — honest is what matters
