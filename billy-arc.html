Billy and the Kid

A friendship that ends the only way impossible things can.

J LemeThompson · LT Publishing · Literary Fiction

About the book

A fable while it is happening. A grief once it is done.

When a 312-year-old leprechaun arrives in suburban Alabama—exiled from his clan and entirely unprepared for central air conditioning—he is taken in by nine-year-old John Dale Greene, who keeps two notebooks: one for things that happen, one for things that should not be able to.

What follows is a summer of pancakes sneaked upstairs, wheelchair rides past murdered crepe myrtles, a wish gone wrong in Clay County, and a friendship between two people trying to understand where they belong.
