A collector's volume,

The debut Belle Époque title gathers the poster art of the Golden Age — travel, entertainment, and commercial design from the era that invented the modern poster as an art form. Each plate is reproduced from high-resolution source material, sequenced and paired with context for a coffee table that wants to be picked up, not just admired.

Printed as a hardcover with the dust jacket the format deserves, sized for a shelf, not a drawer.

What we make

Belle Époque Editions publishes art history in physical form — coffee table volumes built around a single period, movement, or visual tradition. The Golden Age poster volume is the first; more will follow as the catalogue grows.

Each title is sourced, sequenced, and designed with the same care given to any book carrying the LT name — just aimed at a shelf meant to be seen as much as read.

Who's behind it

Belle Époque Editions is an imprint of LT Media, edited and produced from Lisbon. Art history direction and visual curation by Alex; production and editorial by the same team behind LT Publishing and LT Select.

Interested in a future volume, a print from the collection, or press access? Reach us through LT Media.
